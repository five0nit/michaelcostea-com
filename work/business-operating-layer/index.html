Work story · Making operations easier to run

AEH AI-enabled operating layer

CRM, field service, finance, communications and staff workflows connected so work is easier to see, hand over and improve.

01

Business systems

Making work flow
Problem

Lead intake, customer communications, field-service jobs, finance actions, reporting and compliance were distributed across brands and systems.

System

Connected Pipedrive, simPRO, Microsoft Graph, n8n, Google Cloud Run, customer intake, call logging, council imports, job reconciliation, sales planning, compliance, reporting and role-specific tools.

Decision

Automate repeatable low-risk work while preserving named human approval, action receipts, exception reporting and fail-closed boundaries for money, compliance, quality and customer commitments.

Ownership

Operational discovery, requirements, implementation, deployment, observability, governance, adoption, documentation, support and operator handover.

Day-to-day impact

Less double-handling, clearer ownership and faster follow-up. Repeated work moves through connected systems instead of being copied between inboxes, spreadsheets and people.

Operational visibility

Exceptions become easier to see. Dashboards, receipts and reconciliations surface stale leads, missing handoffs, failed actions and work that needs a person’s judgment.

Adoption and handover

Role-specific tools, plain documentation, review steps and operator handover keep the system understandable to the people who use and support it.

What matters

The goal is not automation volume. It is calmer operations: fewer mystery gaps, less chasing, better context at each handoff, and useful tools that fit the work already happening.
