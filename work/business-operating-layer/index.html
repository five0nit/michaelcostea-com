Case 01 · Current business impact

AEH AI-enabled operating layer

CRM, field service, finance, communications and staff workflows connected with receipts, controls and operator handover.

01

Business systems

Current impact
Problem

Lead intake, customer communications, field-service jobs, finance actions, reporting and compliance were distributed across brands and systems.

System

Connected Pipedrive, simPRO, Microsoft Graph, n8n, Google Cloud Run, customer intake, call logging, council imports, job reconciliation, sales planning, compliance, reporting and role-specific tools.

Decision

Automate repeatable low-risk work while preserving named human approval, action receipts, exception reporting and fail-closed boundaries for money, compliance, quality and customer commitments.

Ownership

Operational discovery, requirements, implementation, deployment, observability, governance, adoption, documentation, support and operator handover.

Runtime and output scale

84,400 recorded events—not manual tasks saved. The evidence estate includes workflow and scheduler activity, polls, retries, errors and productive outcomes. Separately, 5,955 records were classified as productive actions, outcomes or completed analyses.

CRM and commercial context

795 unique automation or agent-assisted Pipedrive deal IDs. Within that deduplicated cohort, 67 deals worth A$868k are Won and 158 deals worth A$1.103M remain Open: A$1.97M associated current Won/Open pipeline, not claimed as AI-attributed revenue.

Job, finance and action evidence

Machine logs recorded 124 successful simPRO job-creation actions, 459 HTTP-successful Xero invoice actions, 96 email drafts and 93 Pipedrive writebacks. A$1.73M is nominal invoice-action throughput—not independently reconciled issued, paid or collected revenue.

Capacity model

251 modelled cumulative hours—about 31 working days—released from late March to early August 2026. Recurring control-tower and watchdog workflows annualise to approximately 735 hours/year and A$47.8k/year in gross labour-capacity value. Cumulative and annualised figures are separate lenses and are not additive.
