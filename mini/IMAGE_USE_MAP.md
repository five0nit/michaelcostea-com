## Selection rules

1. Fetch live assetOverrides status and exclude archived IDs plus their exact aliases before selection.
2. Match useWhen and useCases to the content intent before matching a single keyword.
3. Filter to isCanonical=true unless a legacy filename is explicitly required.
4. Respect audience, tone, avoidWhen, and placement before choosing an image.
5. Prefer one semantic hero image plus at most two accent or signoff images.
6. Use real screenshots instead when content needs product proof or exact UI evidence.
7. Always use altText when embedding an asset in public content.
8. Use collectionId=v2-designs when the request specifically calls for the new V2 Designs collection.
