A bit more context

Some of the work behind the projects

Three stories about operations, people and agents—not a scoreboard.

01

Business systems

Making work flow
AEH AI-enabled operating layer

CRM, field service, finance and staff workflows connected with evidence and controls

Problem

CRM intake, customer communication, field-service jobs, finance actions, reporting and compliance were distributed across brands and tools.

System

Pipedrive, simPRO, Microsoft Graph, n8n, Cloud Run, council imports, call logging, job reconciliation, sales planning, dashboards and role-specific tools.

Decision

Automate low-risk repeatable work while preserving named human approval, receipts, exception reporting and fail-closed boundaries.

Ownership

Discovery through implementation, deployment, observability, governance, adoption, documentation and operator handover.

Impact

Less double-handling, clearer ownership, faster follow-up and better exception visibility. The useful part is not activity volume; it is helping people see what needs attention and move work cleanly between systems.

02

Knowledge operations

People + process
Scale + adoption

Knowledge and support operations at national frontline scale

Problem

Duplicated processes, support load and search friction across a large frontline audience.

System

Process redesign, knowledge maintenance, content removal/rewrite, training, communications and reporting.

Decision

Treat documentation quality, ownership, staff adoption and measurement as one operating loop.

Ownership

Operational analysis, process intervention, rollout support and performance visibility.

Impact

Cleaner processes, easier-to-find answers, fewer avoidable support loops and changes that made sense to frontline teams.

03

Agent operations

Agents that behave
Orchestration + control

Visible, permissioned multi-agent and model-routing operations

Problem

Agents, models, accounts and machines create invisible work, auth uncertainty, duplicated context and permission risk.

System

Hermes, Codex/APIs, Claude, local LLM routing, shared state, schedules, watchdogs, usage tracking and operator receipts.

Decision

Route by capability, privacy, latency and cost; fail closed for public, financial and destructive actions.

Ownership

Architecture, implementation, safety model, acceptance tests, runtime monitoring and recovery paths.

Impact

Less invisible automation, clearer handoffs, safer boundaries, and a better chance of knowing whether the agent actually finished the job.
