03 · Adoption path

A 90-day path to owned capability.

Pilot focus: Service exception brief.

01Days 1–30

Discover and baseline

  • Observe the current service-exception workflow.
  • Confirm approved sources, owners and human review.
  • Baseline elapsed time, rework and quality.

Gate: one bounded workflow with an owner and measurable baseline.

02Days 31–60

Prove and coach

  • Run supervised cases with a named champion cohort.
  • Capture failed, edited and escalated outputs.
  • Turn examples into short playbooks and clinics.

Gate: quality improves without hidden review cost or unsafe shortcuts.

03Days 61–90

Scale and hand over

  • Expand only to similar teams and approved patterns.
  • Publish scorecard, exception path and ownership map.
  • Train managers and champions for independent use.

Gate: operational owners can run, measure and improve the pattern.
