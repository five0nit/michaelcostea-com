04 · First 90 days

Start with truth, then earn speed.

Selected hypothesis: Booking-to-workshop exception brief.

01Days 1–30

Map and baseline

  • Confirm executive outcomes, decision rights and technology/service owners.
  • Build the minimum viable architecture, dependency and lifecycle register.
  • Trace booking-to-workshop exceptions and the current approval path.

Gate: one agreed service map, one measured baseline, one named owner.

02Days 31–60

Stabilise and prove

  • Close the highest-value visibility, ownership or recovery evidence gap.
  • Run supervised cases; record edits, failures, escalations and residual effort.
  • Publish service, data and delivery scorecards with honest denominators.

Gate: reliability and quality improve without hidden support or review cost.

03Days 61–90

Scale with controls

  • Sequence renew, integrate, retire and protect decisions by business value and risk.
  • Extend only patterns with repeatable ownership, controls and adoption support.
  • Take the next-quarter roadmap, investment cases and unresolved risks to ELT.

Gate: operators own the pattern; executives can see value, cost, risk and next decision.
